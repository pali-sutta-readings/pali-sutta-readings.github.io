#+begin_quote
*Sabbaso*'ti imaṁ dhammadesanaṁ satthā jetavane viharanto pañcaggadāyakaṁ nāma
brāhmaṇaṁ ārabbha kathesi.

So kira sasse khette ṭhitakāleyeva khettaggaṁ nāma deti, khalakāle khalaggaṁ nāma deti, \\
khalabhaṇḍakāle khalabhaṇḍaggaṁ nāma deti, ukkhalikakāle kumbhaggaṁ nāma deti, \\
pātiyaṁ vaḍḍhitakāle pātaggaṁ nāma detī'ti

imāni pañca aggadānāni deti, sampattassa adatvā nāma na bhuñjati. Tenassa *pañcaggadāyako*'tveva nāmaṁ
ahosi. Satthā tassa ca brāhmaṇiyā cassa tiṇṇaṁ phalānaṁ upanissayaṁ disvā
brāhmaṇassa bhojanavelāyaṁ gantvā dvāre aṭṭhāsi.
#+end_quote

# standing in the field; he gave the first-fruits of the threshing floor at the
# time of threshing; he gave the first-fruits of the heap when the grain was
# piled; he gave the first-fruits of the pot at the time of cooking; and he gave
# the first-fruits of the bowl when the food was served.
#
# He gives these five first-fruit offerings; he never eats without first giving
# to whoever has arrived. Because of that, his name was "Giver of Five
# First-fruits." The Teacher, seeing the potential for the three fruits [of the
# path] in him and his wife, went at the brahmin's mealtime and stood at the
# door.
