/Munana/ is a regular /-ana/ action-noun built on /munāti/, "knows, comprehends, takes the measure of."
The ablative /-to/ is the standard commentarial /nirutti/ formula:
/X-ana-to/ = "he is called Y because of X-ing."
So: a /muni/ is a /muni/ because he /munāti/.
