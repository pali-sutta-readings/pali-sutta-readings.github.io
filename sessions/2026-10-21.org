** Verbal forms of 'going'

√gam･1 a (go) gacchati *[√gam + a + ti]* (+acc) goes; walks; moves; wanders around; travels 

(European sibling cognates: EN 'come', IT 'venire', PT 'vir')

√kam･1 a (go) kamati *[√kam + a + ti]* (+instr) goes; travels; flies (by)  

(not a variant of Pāli √gam: from Sanskrit √kram 'to step')

√yā･1 a (go) yāti *[√yā + a + ti]* (+acc) goes; goes to; goes forward; proceeds; travels
