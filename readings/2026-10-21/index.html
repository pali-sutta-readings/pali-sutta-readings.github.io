    Verbal forms of 'going'

    √gam･1 a (go) gacchati [√gam + a + ti] (+acc) goes; walks; moves; wanders around; travels

    (European sibling cognates: EN 'come', IT 'venire', PT 'vir')

    √kam･1 a (go) kamati [√kam + a + ti] (+instr) goes; travels; flies (by)

    (not a variant of Pāli √gam: from Sanskrit √kram 'to step')

    √yā･1 a (go) yāti [√yā + a + ti] (+acc) goes; goes to; goes forward; proceeds; travels

    Example quotes

    gacchati [√gam + a + ti] goes; walks; moves

    Translate: The faithful man goes to the village with an all-white chariot.

    Sammādiṭṭhipaccayā ca aneke kusalā dhammā bhāvanāpāripūriṁ gacchanti. (AN 10.107)
    aneka (adj) many; various; countless [na > an + eka] adj, from na eka
    pāripūri 1 (fem) fulfilment; completion; maturity (of) [pari + √pūr + i]

    paṭibhānada (adj) giving eloquence; providing wit [paṭibhāna + da] adj, comp

    medhāvī (masc) intelligent man; wise man [√medh + ā + vī], [medhā + vī]
    adhigacchati 1 (pr) gets; finds; acquires; obtains [adhi + gaccha + ti]

    Andhabhūto ayaṁ loko, / tanuk'ettha vipassati;
